# Argo v2.2 signoff waivers

Run: `runs/sram_full6` (tag `argo-v2.2-signoff`). Gate: `./signoff_check.sh sram_full6` passes
(KLayout DRC, LVS, density and antenna all 0, routing DRC 0, hold met on all corners,
typical-corner setup met).

Each waiver lists what, why, scope and owner. Anything outside the scope reopens it.

## W1. Slow-corner setup, WNS -1.38 ns

- **What:** setup fails at nom_slow_1p08V_125C (1.08 V, 125 °C) against the 10 ns clock.
- **Why:** the target is 100 MHz at the typical corner (+2.82 ns slack). At the slow corner the
  chip runs at about 88 MHz (1 / 11.38 ns). The clock comes from an external pin, so a slower
  clock fixes it on the bench. The datasheet states both numbers.
- **Scope:** slow corner only, WNS no worse than -1.5 ns. Hold must stay positive on all corners.
- **Owner:** Rakshith Suresh, 2026-10-23.

## W2. Max slew (66), max cap (7), max fanout (224) at the slow corner

- **What:** OpenROAD electrical checks after routing.
- **Why:** the remaining fanout and cap violations sit on nets with antenna diodes, mainly the
  `mem_wdata` bus, which has about 6 to 10 real loads each and the rest diodes. Capping repair
  wire length at 600 µm cut slew violations from 1,297 to 66 and diodes from 2,086 to 1,266.
  `vdd_sram_pad/pad` (slew) and `clk_pad/p2c` (cap, 2.5 % over) are pad-cell pins and not real
  signal paths. Typical-corner timing includes these nets and has +2.82 ns slack.
- **Scope:** counts no higher than the numbers above. Any violation on a clock net reopens this.
- **Owner:** Rakshith Suresh, 2026-10-23.

## W3. OpenROAD antenna checker reports 239 nets

- **What:** `route__antenna_violation__count` = 239 from OpenROAD's LEF-based checker.
- **Why:** the signoff antenna check is IHP's KLayout deck, which reports 0. OpenROAD uses the
  LEF antenna models, which are more pessimistic than the deck.
- **Scope:** valid only while `klayout__antenna_error__count` = 0.
- **Owner:** Rakshith Suresh, 2026-10-23.

## W4. check_setup: 8 input ports without set_input_delay

- **What:** STA warns that `input_PAD[*]` have no input delay.
- **Why:** these are asynchronous inputs (SPI, UART RX, sram_en strap). They are synchronized
  on chip and declared with `set_false_path -from [get_ports {input_PAD[*]}]` and
  `set_false_path -from [get_ports rst_n_PAD]` in the SDC.
- **Scope:** valid while every input port has a synchronizer or is a static strap.
- **Owner:** Rakshith Suresh, 2026-10-23.

## W5. Gate-level sim initializes the CPU's non-reset flops

- **What:** `run_gls.sh` is run with `PROBE=tb/gls_xinit.svh`, generated by `gls_xinit.py`. It
  holds the D input of every `u_cpu` flop at 0 while the SPI boot loads (CPU in reset) and
  releases it before RUN.
- **Why:** PicoRV32's `mul_counter` has no reset. Synthesis built the bit-0 idle reload as
  `NOT(Q OR NOT Q)`, which is always 0 in silicon but stays X in simulation, so every multiply
  hung in GLS. `tb/gls_probe_mul.svh` shows the counter loading `0011110` and the multiply
  returning the correct result once bit 0 starts from a real value. In silicon the flop powers
  up as 0 or 1 and the first idle cycle loads 0.
- **Scope:** GLS only. The init covers one legal power-up state, not all of them.
- **Evidence:** hello, sa_test, post (sram_en=1) and post (sram_en=0) pass on `sram_full6`.
- **Owner:** Rakshith Suresh, 2026-10-23.

## W6. Static IR drop (closed)

- **Result:** `./irdrop_check.sh sram_full6 0.2`, typical corner, 0.2 toggle rate on every net,
  67 mW total. Sources placed only where the supply pads meet the core ring.
  VPWR worst 28.1 mV (2.3 %), VGND worst 35.6 mV (3.0 %), VDD_SRAM 0.24 mV. All shapes connected
  (PSM-0040) and power_grid_violation = 0.
- **Why it is acceptable:** each rail is under the 5 % (60 mV) budget. Even the sum of both worst
  cases (63.7 mV) leaves the cells at about 1.136 V from a 1.2 V supply, above the 1.08 V the slow
  corner is timed at.
- **Scope:** board supply at least 1.15 V at the VDD pins. Rerun if activity or power rises.
- **Owner:** Rakshith Suresh, 2026-10-23.
